## About

Munafis is a web app for family games inspired by culture, heritage and sports. A human host sets up the games, players and teams. An AI judge watches and listens to real play through video and audio, applies the game rules, and keeps the score with a reason for every result.

The Cycle 3 track requires 3 games: football + 2 original games.

## How it works

1. Choose a game – football or one of the team's original games.
2. Players and teams – the host adds players and saves the teams.
3. Rules and ready – points, time limits, penalties and tie-breaks are shown before the round starts.
4. Round in play – turns, rounds, timers and scores are managed by the app.
5. AI judge results – the game video and audio are uploaded or recorded; the AI judge finds the facts (for example who has the ball, whether a goal was scored, what answer was spoken) and the app applies them to the score.
6. Final results – winner, match facts and reasons are shown (football also names the MVP), and the family can choose another game.

## Features

- Host-first setup for games, players and teams
- AI judging from video and audio, with reasons shown for each result
- Score, turn, round and timer management
- Football with player/ball tracking and an MVP result
- Two original team games suited to AI judging
- Arabic and English, large readable text, high contrast

## Run it

Online (recommended): open https://esoteric-win-arena-pro.base44.app in a modern browser (phone, tablet or laptop). No installation or sign-up request is needed to view it.

Camera and microphone: to record a game directly, allow camera and microphone access when the browser asks. You can also upload an existing game video with audio.

From source: the app is built and hosted on Base44. To work on it, open the project in Base44, or copy the exported source files from this repository into your own environment.

## Use of AI

AI tools were used to understand the problem, plan the work, write code and fix errors. Every suggestion was tested before use, and all AI conversations are included in the consolidated PDF submitted to the competition.

## Limitations

- AI judging depends on camera angle, lighting and audio quality.
- The prototype was built in 3 hours and tested on a small scale only.

## Future work
